Plannings show each block's start and end times on its edges. When times would touch (a phone, short blocks), they alternate between a top and a bottom row, and a time with no room on either is left out; a block being dragged shows its new times beside it. A `times` option (`bar` or `below`) on `week-schedule`, `day-schedule`, `area-roster-view` and `person-lane-track`, and on the My planning card, lists the times as text under each day instead.
