Settings → Heating now shows the heating an area already has in Home Assistant ("Found in Home Assistant: …") and "Set up heating" opens the form pre-filled: the area's thermostat, else its heater switches (a group switch is kept and the physical switches it drives are dropped), and the area's temperature sensor setting, else a temperature sensor in the area. The Rules tab says where to set up heating when no area has any yet.
